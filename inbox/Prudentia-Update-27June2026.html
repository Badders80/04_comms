Back to Maiden Ground

Prudentia broke her maiden at Tauranga. This Saturday she returns to the same track — recovered, sharp in her work, and accepted for Race 7 from Barrier 1. Can she do it again?

A minor front-foot soreness in early June prompted a tactical withdrawal from her 13 June engagement — the right call to protect the longer campaign. Lance O'Sullivan and Andrew Scott kept her fit through swimming before bringing her back to trackwork with protective pads on both front feet. Her movement is fluid again, and recent work — including a partnered grass gallop with stablemate Ribkraka — has her pulling up clean and sharp.

“She’s coming right, but she just ran out of runway to get there this Saturday. We’ve kept her swimming to maintain fitness, and we’ll target Tauranga in two weeks.”
— Andrew Scott, Wexford Stables

She lines up in Race 7 (BM75) over 1400m at 4:21 PM on Saturday, 27 June. Barrier 1, minimum weight 54.0kg, and Masa Hashizume is booked to ride. Ribkraka (Barrier 6) also takes her place for Wexford in the same race.

Prudentia with Masa Hashizume — back together for Saturday’s BM75 at Tauranga.

The yard is pleased with where she sits physically. She has the weight, the draw, a jockey who knows her, and conditions that suit. Now it comes down to racing luck on the day.

  • 54.0kg minimum weight — gives away up to 5.5kg to the top weight on a testing winter track
  • Barrier 1 — Masa can save ground early or search for better going out wide
  • Tauranga — maiden-win venue; she excels on the turning layout and soft-to-heavy footing
  • Saturday 27 June, 4:21 PM — Race 7, BM75, 1400m

We’ll follow up with a full post-race report after Saturday.
